How to Play

  • Swim: WASD / arrows, or move the mouse. On touch, drag anywhere.
  • Dash: Space or the DASH button (costs energy, short cooldown).
  • Pause: Esc or P.
  • Eat anything smaller than you to grow. Bigger red-toothed fish are deadly.
  • A predator shows ! and freezes briefly before it charges. Dash away, or slip past for a near-miss bonus.
  • Chain meals fast to build a combo multiplier.
  • Catch golden fish and glowing jellies for big rewards and energy.
  • Find glowing landmarks: they are safe zones and give exploration bonuses.
  • Ocean currents push you. Ride them, or fight them.
